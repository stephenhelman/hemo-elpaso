# HOEP Master Privacy Policy v2 (Draft)

Nov 23, 2026 · @Stephen

One policy that covers every feature HOEP runs or plans, with each future feature marked "Not yet available" until the admin turns it on. English first; Spanish after board approval.

## How this works

The policy describes every feature in specific terms, including ones not built yet. Each future feature carries a visible label, "Not yet available". The published page reads that label from the same feature toggle the admin account controls. When admin turns a feature on, the label disappears and the section shows "Available since" with the date. The policy is never ahead of or behind the site.

Consents work the same way. Text-message consent can be collected now with honest wording ("once texting starts"). Actions like uploading a letter or applying for help get their own short consent at the moment the family does them, so nothing needs to be asked again later.

**Fix before Puente Live is used at a family event.** Deepgram, the speech-to-text service, keeps audio for model training by default. Puente must send `mip_opt_out=true` on every request, in every mode. With that set, Deepgram keeps audio only while processing it. It costs somewhat more per hour. This policy is written as if the fix is in place.

## Privacy Policy v2

*Hemophilia Outreach of El Paso (HOEP). Effective \[date\]. Version 2.*

HOEP is a nonprofit that supports families affected by bleeding disorders in the El Paso area. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and your choices. Some features below are marked **Not yet available**: we describe them now so you know exactly how they will work before they start. We never sell your information.

### Information we collect

**When you register your household:** your name, email and/or phone number, preferred language, and mailing address if you give it. The people in your household: first name (last name optional), relationship to you, and age group, never a date of birth. If you choose to tell us, whether you or a child in your household has a bleeding disorder, and which one. This is health information, and we treat it with extra care. We also record your consents and when you gave them.

**Adults in your household** confirm their own information. If you list someone 18 or older, we email or call them once. They decide for themselves whether to share health information and whether to consent. If they don't confirm within 30 days, we delete what you entered about them.

**When you RSVP or attend an event:** who in your household is coming, the number of guests, and who checked in.

**When you contact us:** your name, email, phone (optional) and message. Messages go to HOEP's email and are not stored in our database. Please don't include medical details; we will follow up privately.

**When you visit the website:** privacy-friendly page-view statistics that use no cookies and don't identify you, and one cookie that remembers your language. Our hosting provider sees standard technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver the site.

**Site announcements.** *Not yet available.* If you close an announcement, your browser remembers that on your device. It isn’t sent to us.

**Photos at events:** we publish photos only of people who consented to photo use.

**Event recordings and live streams.** *Not yet available.* Some events may be streamed live and recorded so families who can't attend can watch. We announce this on the invitation, at the door and at the start. We point cameras at presenters, not the audience. When someone asks a question, the presenter repeats it rather than showing the person. Families who declined photo use are kept off camera. Recordings are posted as unlisted videos or on the family page, not publicly searchable.

**Live captions and translation (Puente Live).** *Not yet available.* At some events and meetings, HOEP's own app, Puente Live, shows live English and Spanish captions of what is said. Speech is sent to a speech-to-text service and the text to a translation service; neither keeps it beyond processing, except as described under "How long we keep it". HOEP doesn't save the audio. Captions are machine-made and can be wrong; they are not a replacement for a qualified interpreter. A board member can pause captions at any time, for example when someone shares something personal. Board members may download a transcript after an event to help write newsletters or blog posts. We never publish a whole transcript.

**When you sign in to your family page.** *Not yet available.* You sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook. The service shares your name and email with us, and we use the email to find your household. We keep a record of which sign-in is linked to your household. If we can’t find your household, we keep that sign-in record for up to 30 days so the board can help you. We never receive your password for those services. A cookie keeps you signed in.

**When you verify a diagnosis (optional).** *Not yet available.* You can upload a letter from a health care provider confirming a bleeding disorder for you or a child in your household. Only the President and Vice President can open it. We delete the file as soon as it's reviewed and keep only the result: which diagnosis was confirmed, the date, and who reviewed it.

**Text messages.** *Not yet available.* If you opt in, your mobile number and whether you agreed to texts. Texts include event reminders, announcements and sign-in codes. Reply STOP at any time.

**Newsletter.** *Not yet available.* If you subscribe, your email and language. You can unsubscribe in any newsletter.

**Patient stories and blog.** *Not yet available.* If you share your story, what you choose to tell us and any photos you provide, only with your signed permission (a parent or guardian signs for a child). Quotes from events are used without names unless the person gives permission.

**Financial assistance and scholarships.** *Not yet available.* If you apply, what the application asks for, such as the reason, the amount and supporting documents, plus a record of any payment. These programs require a verified diagnosis.

**Polls and questions at events.** *Not yet available.* Your answers to live polls are anonymous. Questions you submit may be shown on screen without your name.

**Community Q&A.** *Not yet available.* Questions and answers you post, which a board member reviews before they appear. Please don't post health details you wouldn't want others to see.

**Online donations.** *Not yet available.* A payment company processes donations. HOEP receives your name, email, the amount and the date for receipts. We never see or store your full card number.

**Volunteer sign-up.** *Not yet available.* Your name, contact information, availability and the events you volunteer for.

### How we use it

- To review new registrations before sending invitations.
- To invite you to events. Some events are for specific groups, such as families affected by a particular condition or children of a certain age. Invitations never say why you were invited or mention anyone's diagnosis.
- To manage RSVPs, send confirmations and reminders, and check people in.
- To answer your messages.
- *When available:* to let you see your household's information and manage your photo, text and newsletter choices on your family page; to confirm a diagnosis if you choose to verify it; to send texts and newsletters you opted into; to review assistance applications.
- To improve our programs using overall counts, never individual records.

We do not use your information for advertising. We do not sell, rent or trade it. **Sponsors never receive names or contact details.** They see totals only, such as how many families attended.

### Verifying a diagnosis

*Not yet available.* Verification is optional. You can register, RSVP and use your family page without it.

If you don't verify: 60 days after your household is asked to, we stop including you in invitations to events meant for a specific diagnosis. You still get general invitations, and you can still RSVP to any event you have a link for. You can verify any time and be included again. Financial assistance and scholarships require verification.

How it works: you upload a letter, the President or Vice President reviews it, and we delete the file once they decide, whether or not they approve it. If they can't approve it, we tell you a general reason and you can try again. We never ask for medical records beyond the letter, and we never share the letter or the result with other organizations.

### Who can see it

Only HOEP board members who need it for their role, with access limited by role. Health information, including diagnosis letters, is visible only to the President and Vice President. Other board members may see your contact details and household members to confirm your registration; they never see health information. Board members sign in with secure accounts, and every access to family records is logged. The account that manages website settings cannot see family information.

We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

### Your consents

When you register, you agree that HOEP may keep your information and contact you about events and services. Photo and video use is separate and optional, and so are texts and the newsletter. Some actions, like uploading a letter or applying for assistance, ask for a short consent at that moment. You can change any consent by contacting us. Withdrawing consent stops invitations and other non-essential messages; we still send confirmations and reminders for an RSVP you keep.

### Children

Only adults (18 or older) can register or sign in. A parent or legal guardian may include their children. See our Children and Age Policy.

### Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what we have about you and your household; correct it; delete it completely, including any verification result; remove a photo, recording or quote of you or your child; or stop contacting you. Contact privacy@hemoelpaso.org or (915) 539-7353. We may ask a question to confirm it's you, and we respond within 30 days.

### Security

Encrypted connections, role-limited access for board members, private links that can't be guessed, and no health information in emails, links or QR codes. *When available:* diagnosis letters are stored privately and deleted after review. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affected your information, we would notify you as the law requires.

### Changes to this policy

When a feature marked "Not yet available" starts, this page updates to show the date. If we change how we use information you already gave us, we'll tell you and ask for your consent again.

## Consent v3 text

v1 and v2 stay in the code unchanged for older records. New consents record v3. Data and contact consent and the 18+ attestation carry over from v2 as they are.

**Photos, video and recordings (replaces the media consent; optional, at registration)**

> I agree that HOEP may take and publish photos and video of me and the minors in my household at HOEP events, including event recordings and live streams. HOEP will not name us without asking. I can withdraw this at any time and ask HOEP to remove a photo or recording.

**Text messages (new; optional, at registration and on the family page)**

> I agree that HOEP may text me at this number about events, reminders, announcements and sign-in codes, once HOEP starts sending texts. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to stop, HELP for help. Consent is not required to take part in HOEP.

**Newsletter (new; optional)**

> Send me HOEP's newsletter by email. I can unsubscribe in any newsletter.

**Diagnosis verification (new; shown at upload, required before the upload button works)**

> I agree that HOEP's President or Vice President may read the letter I am uploading, only to confirm the diagnosis it names. HOEP will delete the letter when they decide and keep only the result (which diagnosis, the date, and who reviewed it). If the letter is about a child in my household, I am that child's parent or legal guardian.

**Quotes (notice on the RSVP page, not a checkbox)**

> HOEP may quote things said at this event in its newsletter or website without names. We will ask first before using anyone's name.

**Story release (new; signed through DocuSign for each story)**

> I give HOEP permission to publish my story, and any photos I provide, on its website, newsletter and social media, in English and Spanish. I have reviewed the final version. I can ask HOEP to remove it at any time. \[If about a minor:\] I am this child's parent or legal guardian.

**Speaker release (new; presenters and sponsor representatives)**

> I agree that HOEP may record, caption, translate and publish my presentation, including my voice and image.

## Event notices for recordings and captions

Use these whenever an event is recorded, streamed or captioned.

**Invitation and RSVP page line**

> This event will be recorded, streamed and shown with live English and Spanish captions. Cameras face the presenters, not the audience. Tell us at check-in if you'd like to stay off camera.

**Door sign**

> This event is being recorded and live streamed, with machine-made captions in English and Spanish. Cameras face the presenters. Want to stay off camera? Tell the check-in table. Photos are only published with permission.

**Spoken opener (presenter or host)**

> Welcome. Today's event is being recorded and streamed, and the captions you see are made by a computer, so they may not be perfect. The cameras are on our speakers, not on you. When you ask a question, I'll repeat it before answering, so you don't have to be on camera. If you share something personal, we'll pause the captions. Thank you for being here.

**Tech checklist**

- Deepgram opt-out is on in Puente (`mip_opt_out=true`)
- Cameras on the presenter; audience out of frame
- NO PHOTOS families seated out of frame
- Pause captions and mute the stream when someone shares something personal
- YouTube video set to unlisted
